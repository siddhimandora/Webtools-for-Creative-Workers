Initially the process felt long and there was a lot of imposster syndrome while creating original work. It felt painful and never felt complete.
There was a drive to keep pusing the song to make it better and over produce it to make it more original. There was more anxiety in creating
and it was more difficult to complete the song. Now, There is more sureity in the process. The idea is to keep it as simple as possible with more
soul and try to not over produce the song. The process also feels more natural and less forced.
